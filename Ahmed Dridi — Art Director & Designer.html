I’m Ahmed Dridi, an Art Director and Senior Graphic Designer based in Tunisia. My creative journey started as a freelancer in 2017.

I bring together branding, graphic design, UI/UX, motion design and video editing. My experience spans projects in Tunisia and Dubai, and remote collaboration with a French company.

I enjoy turning a brief into a clear visual direction — then carrying that idea through every format, down to the final detail.
